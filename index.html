Where we stand

What is locked, what is assumed, what is open

Every figure in this dossier carries one of three statuses. The scenario is not chosen yet, so all three scenarios are shown side by side.

Locked

Decisions and documents

  • 12 slices per Pullman brioche, 1,067.05 g of dough per mould, 5 % production reserve.
  • Volume steps of 100, 250, 500 and 700 portions. At 700 sales: 62 brioches, 66.16 kg of dough, 735 portions with reserve.
  • Oven: 24 moulds per batch (6 of 10 levels used, 4 moulds per level), validated by trial. Three batches at peak.
  • Proofer: 18 levels, 12 usable, 5 moulds per level, 60 moulds; two 2 h 30 cycles.
  • Mixer: 30 kg of dough, three kneads at peak, one machine.
  • Safety stock of 2 days: 124 brioches.
  • The initial 150 to 200 k SAR envelope is not a constraint. Equipment only.
Assumption to confirm

Technical hypotheses

  • 62 moulds and 60 aluminium trays in the CAPEX; the real stock depends on rotations.
  • Pullman mould of 32 × 11 cm outside, 5 per 60 × 40 tray.
  • Level pitch of 12.5 to 13 cm in the proofer.
  • The 30 kg mixer takes 22 kg of enriched dough.
  • Two cooling racks (Step 5 says 2 to 3).
  • Slicer thickness of 25 mm, to be tried on a Pullman loaf.
Not validated

Models, prices, suppliers

  • No supplier quote received. Prices are references or estimates with a reliability level.
  • Mixer (38,500 SAR) and proofer (41,200 SAR) are extrapolated estimates.
  • Scenario A, B or C: no choice yet (Anas and Ahmed).
  • B2B brioche: no supplier, no specification, no price. None is invented.
  • Site power, water, drainage and extraction: no survey yet.

Water, drainage, extraction

  • Hot water at 60 °C minimum for the dishwasher; a commercial water heater
  • 4 water points in B2B; 6 with the oven and the proofer
  • Floor drains and a grease trap, which is mandatory
  • A hood over the cooking line and one over the dishwasher; for the oven, a hood that is excluded from the current price

Open choices

  • Electric plancha (counted at 5 kW) or gas. Gas brings Civil Defence rules: cylinders outside, gas valve linked to the extraction.
  • Voltage: the schedule lists 400 V and 230 V. Saudi supply is 60 Hz, so the supplier's matching version must be specified.
  • Check the supply capacity with the electricity company early: a new supply can add 4 to 8 weeks.

Storage sizing

Rule in the SOP manual: two peak days of frozen brioche, 124 loaves of 3 L each.

  • 1 day (62 loaves): 370 to 470 L, a 1-door freezer
  • 2 days (124): 740 to 930 L, a 2-door freezer
  • 3 days (186): 1,100 to 1,400 L, a full 2-door freezer or a walk-in freezer
  • Fresh dairy and eggs: about 56 kg a day at 700; two days fit one 1-door refrigerator

Operating framework

Receiving, storage, cleaning, stock, waste, food safety

This completes the SOP manual, which covers production and key controls. Temperatures reuse the manual's key-controls page and remain proposals to validate in the site's HACCP plan. The exact local rules (Balady, SFDA) must be checked against the official texts.

Receiving
  • Deliveries outside service hours; check the vehicle, the packaging and the dates.
  • Probe temperature: chilled up to 5 °C, frozen −18 °C or colder (proposed). Refuse anything thawed, damaged or out of date.
  • Frozen brioche (scenarios A and C): checked at every delivery, which is every second day.
Storage and traceability
  • First in, first out. Label: product, batch, manufacture date, use-by date, allergens, operator.
  • Batters, creams and sensitive sauces at 4 °C or below; frozen at −18 °C or below.
  • Record the refrigerator temperature at every service opening.
Cleaning and sanitation
  • A written plan per zone: frequency, product, dilution, person responsible.
  • Clean, rinse, disinfect, rinse. Colour-coded cloths. Signed visual check at the end of service.
  • Hood and grease filters on a fixed cleaning schedule.
Stock management
  • Daily count of sensitive items (eggs, milk, cream, butter); weekly for dry goods.
  • A reorder threshold per product. Orders based on the next day's volume plus the 5 % reserve already locked.
Waste
  • Sort organic waste, packaging and used oil. Pedal bins are already in the CAPEX.
  • Remove waste at the end of each service. Weigh losses if actual food cost is to be tracked.
Food safety and HACCP
  • French toast core at 74 °C or above. Hot holding at 63 °C or above. Batter tray changed every 2 hours.
  • Cooked preparations cooled to 10 °C or below within 2 hours, then 4 °C or below.
  • Poached egg with a runny yolk: pasteurised eggs, to be validated in the HACCP plan before opening.
  • No shelf life is validated yet. Each must come from a trial, including freezing and thawing.
Staff and health certificates
  • A health certificate and a food-safety course accredited by the SFDA for every employee.
  • Clean uniform, hand washing, gloves changed between products.
Allergens and menu labelling
  • Since July 2025 the SFDA requires menus to show calories, fat, sugar, sodium, caffeine and 14 allergens.
  • The brioche formula declares gluten, milk and eggs. Soy in the improver is still to be confirmed with the supplier.
